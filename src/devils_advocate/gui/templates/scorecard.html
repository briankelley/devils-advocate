Methodology

  • Deterministic pass over review ledgers — no model calls, fully reproducible.
  • Scored set: reviews with real model reviewers and at least one governance-resolved finding. Spec mode, e2e/test harness runs, and synthetic stub reviews are excluded.
  • "Accepted" = auto-accepted, accepted, partial, or human-overridden — survival through governance, which also reflects author agreeableness, not ground truth.
  • Conviction is scored once per finding group, from the reviewer's own round-2 verdict on its contested findings.
  • Costs come from per-review role cost records; early ledgers predate them, so cost columns undercount the oldest months.
